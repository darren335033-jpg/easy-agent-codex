PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES · 15 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy & enquiries.

BlackHole Money is a website operated under the Easy Agent Agency name. This page explains how the website's current enquiry process works.

What happens to your details?

When you fill in the enquiry form, your browser prepares a draft message in your own email application. The website itself does not submit or store the information on a contact-form server. You choose whether to send the email. If you send it, the information you include reaches our nominated business email inbox and will be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss requested services.

Hosting and third parties

The hosting provider may process standard connection information to serve the site and maintain service security. This version of the page does not deliberately set analytics cookies or embed third-party advertising or payment trackers. Your email provider may process information when you send your enquiry.

Payments and offers

Pricing shown on the main website is indicative. No automatic card payment, checkout or subscription is processed by this website. Any offer, pricing and service arrangement must be confirmed directly with Easy Agent Agency before an engagement.

Questions and requests

Contact Devops@zohomail.com.au for enquiries about the personal information sent to us by email, including access or deletion requests.
